# 01 — Business Research

## Project

**Google Ads Search Campaign — ATS Happy Trails**

## Business

**Developer:** ATS HomeKraft  
**Industry:** Real Estate  
**Market:** Residential Real Estate  
**Project:** ATS Happy Trails  
**Location:** Sector 10, Greater Noida West, Uttar Pradesh, India

## Business Overview

ATS HomeKraft is a real estate development company operating under the ATS Group. The company focuses on residential projects and aims to provide quality housing with planned communities, modern amenities and customer-focused development.

## Business Objective

The primary business objective for this Google Ads campaign is to generate qualified enquiries from potential property buyers and encourage interested prospects to schedule a site visit.

## Marketing Objective

The Google Ads campaign will focus on capturing high-intent users who are actively searching for residential properties in Greater Noida West and related project-specific searches.

## Unique Selling Points

- Established real estate developer
- Residential project in Greater Noida West
- 3 BHK + Study configuration
- Modern residential amenities
- Location advantage within Greater Noida West
- Project-specific search demand

## Primary Marketing Goal

Generate qualified property enquiries and potential site-visit leads through Google Search Ads.

## Campaign Objective

**Lead Generation**

The campaign will target users with strong purchase intent and direct them to the official project page for further information and enquiry.
