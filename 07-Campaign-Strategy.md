# 07 — Campaign Strategy

## Campaign Overview

**Campaign Name:** ATS Happy Trails — Search Campaign

**Platform:** Google Ads

**Campaign Type:** Search

**Primary Objective:** Lead Generation

**Secondary Objective:** Generate qualified site-visit enquiries

---

## Campaign Goal

The primary goal is to reach users actively searching for residential properties in Greater Noida West and generate qualified enquiries for ATS Happy Trails.

The campaign will prioritize search intent and lead quality over generating large volumes of low-intent traffic.

---

## Target Location

The campaign will target:

- Noida
- Greater Noida
- Delhi
- Ghaziabad
- Faridabad
- Gurgaon / Gurugram

The primary focus will remain on users interested in properties in Greater Noida West.

---

## Target Audience

### Primary Audience

- Age: Approximately 28–55
- Homebuyers
- Families
- Property upgraders
- Residential property investors
- NCR property seekers

### Search Intent

The campaign will prioritize users searching for:

- ATS Happy Trails
- ATS Happy Trails Greater Noida
- 3 BHK flats Greater Noida West
- Flats for sale Greater Noida West
- Premium apartments Greater Noida West

---

---

## Bidding Strategy

### Initial Strategy

**Maximize Clicks**

The campaign will initially focus on collecting relevant search traffic because there is no historical conversion data available for this practice campaign.

### Future Strategy

After sufficient conversion data is collected, the campaign could be evaluated for a conversion-focused bidding strategy such as:

- Maximize Conversions
- Target CPA

---

## Landing Page Strategy

All ads will direct users to the official ATS Happy Trails project page:

https://homekraft.in/ats-happy-trails/

The landing page should provide relevant project information and clear enquiry or site-visit opportunities.

---

## Conversion Strategy

Potential primary conversions:

- Lead enquiry
- Site visit request
- Brochure request
- Contact form submission

Potential secondary conversions:

- Phone call
- WhatsApp enquiry
- Engagement with project information

---

## Optimization Strategy

The campaign will be optimized using:

- Search Terms Report
- CTR
- CPC
- Conversion Rate
- Cost per Lead
- Keyword performance
- Ad performance
- Negative keywords

Poorly relevant search terms will be excluded through negative keywords.

High-performing keywords and ads can receive greater budget allocation.

---

## Testing Strategy

The campaign will test:

### Keyword Testing

- Project-specific keywords
- Configuration keywords
- Location keywords
- Premium property keywords

### Ad Testing

- Project-focused messaging
- Location-focused messaging
- Configuration-focused messaging
- Site-visit-focused messaging

---

## Success Criteria

The campaign will be evaluated using:

- Relevant clicks
- CTR
- CPC
- Conversion rate
- Cost per lead
- Lead quality
- Site-visit enquiries

The main success indicator is not simply traffic volume but the ability to generate relevant property enquiries.

---

## Strategic Conclusion

The campaign uses a high-intent Search strategy to connect ATS Happy Trails with users actively researching or considering residential property in Greater Noida West.

Tightly themed ad groups, relevant keywords, strong ad messaging and continuous search-term optimization will be used to improve campaign efficiency.
