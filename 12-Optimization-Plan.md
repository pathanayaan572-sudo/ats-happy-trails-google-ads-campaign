# 12 — Optimization Plan

## Objective

The optimization strategy is designed to improve lead quality, reduce wasted advertising spend and increase the efficiency of the Google Search campaign.

---

# 1. Search Terms Optimization

The Search Terms Report will be reviewed regularly.

### Actions

- Identify irrelevant searches
- Add irrelevant terms as negative keywords
- Identify new high-intent search terms
- Add valuable search terms as keywords
- Separate brand and generic intent where necessary

---

# 2. Keyword Optimization

Keywords will be evaluated based on:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- CTR
- CPC
- Conversions
- Conversion Rate
- Cost Per Conversion

### Actions

High-performing keywords may receive more budget.

Low-relevance or consistently poor-performing keywords may be paused or adjusted.

---

# 3. Ad Optimization

The two Responsive Search Ads will be evaluated based on:

- CTR
- Conversion Rate
- Lead quality
- Engagement

### Testing Areas

- Headlines
- Descriptions
- Calls to action
- Project benefits
- Location messaging
- Configuration messaging

Examples of CTA testing:

- Book A Site Visit
- Enquire Now
- Request Project Details
- Explore The Project

---

# 4. Negative Keyword Optimization

Negative keywords will be continuously expanded based on search-term data.

Examples of potentially irrelevant searches:

- Jobs
- Rent
- PG
- Internship
- Free
- Cheap
- Unrelated property types

This helps reduce wasted clicks.

---

# 5. Location Optimization

Performance will be reviewed by location.

If one location generates better-quality leads, budget allocation can be adjusted accordingly.

The campaign can also evaluate whether Greater Noida West-focused targeting performs better than broader NCR targeting.

---

# 6. Budget Optimization

Budget will be allocated based on performance.

### Increase budget when:

- Qualified leads are being generated
- Cost per lead is acceptable
- Conversion rate is stable
- Search intent is strong

### Reduce budget when:

- Traffic is irrelevant
- Cost per lead becomes inefficient
- Conversion quality is poor
- Keywords consistently underperform

---

# 7. Landing Page Optimization

The landing page should be reviewed for:

- Mobile usability
- Page speed
- Clear project information
- Strong calls to action
- Easy enquiry form
- Phone/contact options
- Relevant information matching the ad

The landing page should maintain strong alignment between the search query, ad message and page content.

---

# 8. Conversion Optimization

Primary conversions should be tracked accurately.

Potential conversions:

- Enquiry form submission
- Site visit request
- Brochure request
- Phone call
- WhatsApp enquiry

Conversion tracking should be verified before using conversion-based bidding.

---

# 9. Scaling Strategy

Once consistent qualified conversions are available:

1. Identify top-performing keywords.
2. Identify top-performing locations.
3. Identify top-performing ads.
4. Shift more budget toward high-performing segments.
5. Test additional relevant keywords.
6. Consider conversion-focused bidding.

Budget should be increased gradually based on performance.

---

# 10. Performance Review

The campaign should be reviewed regularly using:

- CTR
- CPC
- Conversion Rate
- Cost Per Lead
- Search Terms
- Keyword performance
- Ad performance
- Lead quality
