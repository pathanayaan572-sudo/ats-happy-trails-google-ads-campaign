# 06 — Keyword Research

## Campaign

**Campaign:** ATS Happy Trails — Google Search  
**Objective:** Generate qualified property enquiries and site-visit leads.

## Keyword Strategy

The keyword strategy focuses on users showing high purchase intent for ATS Happy Trails, 3 BHK properties and residential properties in Greater Noida West.

The campaign will initially use Exact Match and Phrase Match keywords to maintain control over search intent.

## Negative Keyword Strategy

Negative keywords will be reviewed and expanded using the Search Terms Report.

Queries that generate irrelevant clicks or do not match the campaign's purchase intent will be added to the negative keyword list.

---

# Keyword Selection Criteria

Keywords were selected based on:

1. Relevance to the project
2. Residential purchase intent
3. Location relevance
4. Configuration relevance
5. Commercial intent
6. Potential lead-generation value

## Match Type Strategy

### Exact Match

Used for highly specific and high-intent searches where greater control is required.

### Phrase Match

Used to capture relevant variations while maintaining reasonable control over search intent.

### Broad Match

Not used as the primary starting strategy. It may be tested later after sufficient conversion data is available.

## Conclusion

The keyword strategy is designed to capture users searching specifically for ATS Happy Trails as well as users actively looking for 3 BHK and residential properties in Greater Noida West.
