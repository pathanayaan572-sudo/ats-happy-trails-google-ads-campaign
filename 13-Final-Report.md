# 14. Challenges Faced

### Challenge 1 — Selecting Relevant Keywords

Real-estate searches can contain both high-intent and low-intent queries.

**Solution:** Use tightly themed ad groups and Exact/Phrase Match keywords.

### Challenge 2 — Irrelevant Traffic

Broad property searches can generate unrelated searches such as rentals, jobs or low-intent queries.

**Solution:** Use negative keywords and regularly review the Search Terms Report.

### Challenge 3 — Competitive Market

Greater Noida West contains multiple competing residential projects.

**Solution:** Use project-specific, configuration-specific and location-specific messaging.

### Challenge 4 — No Historical Conversion Data

A new campaign does not initially have enough conversion history.

**Solution:** Start with a controlled bidding strategy and collect performance data before moving toward conversion-focused bidding.
