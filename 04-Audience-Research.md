# 04 — Audience Research

## Primary Audience

The primary audience consists of people actively looking to purchase a residential property in Greater Noida West.

## Demographic Profile

**Age:** Approximately 28–55 years

**Location:**
- Noida
- Greater Noida
- Delhi
- Ghaziabad
- Faridabad
- Gurgaon / Gurugram
- Other NCR locations

**Income Profile:** Middle to upper-middle and high-income households with the financial capacity to consider premium residential property.

## Audience Segments

### 1. End-Users

People looking to purchase a home for themselves and their families.

**Intent:** High

**Common needs:**
- Larger apartment
- Good location
- Family-oriented amenities
- Connectivity
- Developer trust
- Long-term livability

### 2. Property Investors

People considering residential property as a long-term investment.

**Intent:** Medium to High

**Common interests:**
- Location growth
- Infrastructure development
- Property appreciation potential
- Rental potential
- Developer reputation

### 3. Upgraders

Existing homeowners looking for a larger or better residential property.

**Intent:** High

**Common needs:**
- 3 BHK + Study
- Better amenities
- Better connectivity
- Modern residential environment

## Search Behaviour

High-intent users may search for:

- ATS Happy Trails
- ATS Happy Trails Greater Noida
- 3 BHK flats Greater Noida West
- flats for sale Greater Noida West
- premium apartments Greater Noida West
- ATS Happy Trails price
- ATS Happy Trails floor plan
- ATS Happy Trails brochure

## Audience Pain Points

Potential buyers may be concerned about:

- Property price
- Developer credibility
- Location and connectivity
- Construction quality
- Possession timeline
- Amenities
- Apartment configuration
- Investment potential
- Availability

## Audience Motivation

Key motivations include:

- Buying a family home
- Upgrading to a larger home
- Moving to a developing NCR location
- Investing in residential property
- Access to modern amenities
- Long-term value

## Campaign Audience Strategy

Since this is a Google Search campaign, keyword intent will be more important than broad demographic targeting.

The campaign will prioritize users who demonstrate strong purchase intent through their search queries.

## Conversion Goal

The desired actions are:

- Lead enquiry
- Brochure request
- Project information request
- Site visit enquiry

## Conclusion

The campaign should focus on high-intent users rather than trying to reach the entire Greater Noida West population. Search queries related to the project, property type and location will be used to identify users who are closer to making a property purchase decision.
